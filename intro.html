Paul Arthur (commonly known as DJ Paul Arthur) is a Miami-born dance music producer and video game music composer known for his melodic sound, and is featured in outlets like Miami New Times and on editorial playlists like YouTube Music’s Chill Dance. Stay tuned for Endless Nights, the sequel to Endless Bloom!

FRL Oculus Launch Pad alumni and Symphonic Partner artist.
